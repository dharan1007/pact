THE PROBLEM

Browser agents are becoming capable faster than permission models are becoming precise.

Today, an agent often receives a session, a credential, or a broad tool and is trusted to “do the right thing.” That is a poor security boundary for consequential actions.

PACT changes the unit of authority from access to an application to permission for one exact transaction.

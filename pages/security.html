TRUST MODEL

Trust without blind access

PACT does not ask you to trust an agent's judgment after giving it broad permissions. It constrains what can happen before authority exists and verifies what happened afterward.

01

Exact-plan authorization

Approval binds to a SHA-256 digest of the precise effects, constraints and canonical base version. Any substitution invalidates authority.

PREVENTS: plan swapping after approval
02

Short-lived capability lease

Commit authority exists only for the approved transaction and expires automatically.

PREVENTS: durable ambient privilege
03

Stale-state rejection

If canonical state changes after preview, the approved transaction cannot commit against the new world.

PREVENTS: acting on obsolete assumptions
04

Negative invariants

PACT verifies what must not change—billing and deletion in the reference transaction—not only intended positive effects.

PREVENTS: collateral side effects
05

Fail-closed persistence

If durable storage fails after an in-memory operation, PACT restores the pre-operation snapshot rather than leaving ghost state.

PREVENTS: memory/durable divergence
06

Tamper-evident receipt chain

Receipts and audit entries are independently hashed and linked so post-hoc mutation is detected.

PREVENTS: unverifiable success claims
